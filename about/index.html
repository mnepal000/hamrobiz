About Nepal in DMV

Nepal in DMV is a community directory of Nepali-owned businesses in the DMV [Washington, DC, Maryland and Virginia] area. It lists 158 businesses across 15 categories and 60 cities, from restaurants and groceries to home care, realtors, immigration lawyers and remittance services.

Listings are compiled from public sources and community suggestions, and each one carries the details a neighbor needs: address, phone, website, a map, and an honest note when ownership could not be confirmed.
